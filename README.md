# Clinical Co-Pilot AI
### Patient-Safe Medical Explanations using Generative AI

Clinical Co-Pilot AI is a Generative AI system designed to convert complex doctor-written clinical notes into safe, patient-friendly explanations without providing diagnoses or treatment decisions.

---

## 🔍 Problem
Clinical notes are written for healthcare professionals, not patients. This creates confusion, anxiety, and poor understanding—especially in multilingual and low-resource settings.

---

## 💡 Solution
Clinical Co-Pilot AI converts clinical notes into:
- A concise clinical summary (English)
- A simple patient explanation (English)
- A patient explanation in Roman Urdu
- A clear medical safety disclaimer

---

## ⚙️ How It Works
- Google Gemini for reasoning and language understanding
- Structured prompt pipeline with strict safety constraints
- Clear separation between clinical information and patient explanations
- Gradio-based interactive demo

---

## 🛡️ Safety by Design
- No diagnosis or treatment recommendations
- Controlled language to reduce hallucinations
- Explicit medical disclaimer in all outputs
