# 🩺 Clinical Co-Pilot AI  
### Patient-Safe Medical Explanations using Generative AI

Clinical Co-Pilot AI is a **human-centered Generative AI system** designed to convert complex, doctor-written clinical notes into **safe, patient-friendly explanations** — without providing diagnoses or treatment decisions.

This project focuses on **responsible AI**, **medical safety**, and **multilingual accessibility**, especially for low-resource healthcare settings.

---

## 🚩 Problem
Clinical notes are written for healthcare professionals, not patients.  
This often causes:
- Confusion and anxiety
- Poor understanding of medical conditions
- Low treatment adherence  
— especially in multilingual and low-resource environments.

---

## 💡 Solution
Clinical Co-Pilot AI transforms clinical notes into:

- ✅ A concise **clinical summary** (English)
- ✅ A **simple patient-friendly explanation** (English)
- ✅ A **Roman Urdu explanation** for accessibility
- ✅ A **clear medical safety disclaimer**

> ⚠️ The system is **non-diagnostic by design** and does not recommend treatments.

---

## ⚙️ How It Works
- Uses **Google Gemini** for reasoning and language understanding
- Structured **prompt pipeline** with strict safety constraints
- Clear separation between:
  - Clinical information
  - Patient-facing explanations
- **Gradio-based interactive demo** for real-time use

---

## 🛡️ Safety by Design
- ❌ No diagnosis or treatment recommendations
- 🔒 Controlled language to reduce hallucinations
- 📋 Explicit medical safety disclaimers
- 🧠 Designed for assistive, not decision-making use
